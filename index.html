Interpretability, in one minute

A language model turns words into long lists of numbers, passes them through dozens of layers, and produces the next word. It works well, but nobody wrote down how it works.

Interpretability research opens the model up and looks at those hidden numbers to explain what it is doing. Every study makes three choices, and they are the columns of the map:

  1. Ask a question about the model.
  2. Pick a tool to look inside.
  3. Point it at a part of the model: a layer, a neuron, a feature, a circuit.
